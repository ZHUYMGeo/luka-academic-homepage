About Me

Hello! I am Yimin Zhu, a Ph.D. student in the Department of Geomatics Engineering at the University of Calgary. My research focuses on hyperspectral image processing , land use and land cover mapping, and generative models for inverse problems , including spectral unmixing and wildfire prediction.

Featured Research

Hyperspectral Image Understanding

Developing spatial-spectral representation learning, Mamba-based architectures, clustering-aware token modeling, and spectral unmixing methods for hyperspectral imagery.

Generative Models for Remote Sensing

Exploring diffusion and generative models for hyperspectral image synthesis, inverse problems, small-sample learning, and physically meaningful remote sensing applications.

Wildfire Risk Modeling

Studying data-driven wildfire prediction, uncertainty, land-cover-aware probability modeling, and interpretable spatiotemporal risk analysis.
